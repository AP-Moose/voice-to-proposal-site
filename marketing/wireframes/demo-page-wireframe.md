# Demo Page Wireframe

## Goal
Show the workflow in sequence and make the product feel low-friction.

## Structure
1. Intro headline and short promise
2. 5 to 7 screenshot sequence
3. Short captions under each step
4. Optional embedded walkthrough video later
5. CTA to book a live demo

## Sequence
- Customer info entry
- Job detail capture
- Captured transcript or notes
- Proposal draft
- Email draft
- Save/send outcome
