# Homepage Wireframe

## Visual thesis
Warm, field-ready, and practical. The homepage should feel like a trusted contractor tool, not a software startup.

## Content plan
- Hero: flagship outcome and real product screens
- Support: pain points and simple 3-step workflow
- Detail: before/after, benefits, audience fit, screenshot demo
- Final CTA: book a walkthrough

## Interaction thesis
- Hero phones drift slightly into place on load
- Sections reveal with simple upward motion
- CTA buttons lift subtly on hover
