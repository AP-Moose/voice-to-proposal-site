FAQ

Straight answers.

Who owns my proposals, photos and videos?

You do. Proposals are saved as Word docs in your own Google Drive, and job footage and finished posts go into a ProLynk folder there too. ProLynk can only see the files it creates in your Drive, not the rest of it.

Does anything send or post without me?

No. You check every proposal before it sends, and you approve every post. ProLynk doesn't post to your Facebook or Instagram by itself: approved posts come as ready-to-post files with the caption, and you put them up.

Does it work on my phone?

That's where it's built to live. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. No app store needed. It works on a laptop too.

Is there a contract?

No. Try it free for 14 days, then it's $49 a month. Cancel anytime.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don't upgrade, you can't create new proposals or posts. Everything you made is still in your Google Drive.

Does it use AI?

Yes. AI (OpenAI) drafts the proposal from your voice note and plans posts from your footage. You review all of it before anything goes to a customer or online.
