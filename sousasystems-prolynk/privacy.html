Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated November 2026.

ProLynk is an app for home-improvement contractors made by SousaSystems ("we", "us"). This page explains what data ProLynk uses, why, and how to get it deleted. We try to keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Account info: your name, email address and password (stored hashed), and your business details you type into your Brand Business profile (business name, phone, license number, towns, services, logo).
  • Job and customer info you enter: customer names, email and job addresses, your voice notes and their transcripts, and job notes.
  • Job media you upload: photos and videos of your jobs.
  • Usage and attribution: basic logs (like IP address, device and browser type) and how you found us (for example the ad or link you came from, via utm parameters).

Google account access

If you connect your Google account, ProLynk asks for two permissions only:

  • Google Drive, files ProLynk creates (drive.file): ProLynk saves your proposals, job media and finished posts into a ProLynk folder in your Drive. It can only see and change files it created or that you open with it, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Gmail, send email (gmail.send): ProLynk sends proposal emails you approve, from your Gmail address. It cannot read your inbox.

ProLynk's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell Google user data, use it for ads, or let humans read it except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires.

You can remove ProLynk's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files stay in your Drive.

AI processing

To write proposals and plan posts, ProLynk sends the relevant text, voice audio and job image thumbnails to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI processes it to return the result to us. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train their models by default. Don't put anything in a voice note you wouldn't put in a proposal.

Payments

Subscriptions are billed by Stripe. Stripe handles your card details; we never see or store your full card number. We receive your billing status (for example: trial, active, canceled).

Cookies and ads tracking

The app uses a sign-in cookie to keep you logged in. This website and the app's sign-up page may use the X (Twitter) pixel to measure whether our ads work (page visits and sign-ups). It doesn't load if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. We don't sell your personal information.

Who we share data with

Only the service providers that run ProLynk: hosting and database providers, Google (Drive and Gmail, on your behalf), OpenAI (AI processing), Stripe (billing), and X (ad measurement, as above). We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Files in your Google Drive are yours and stay there unless you delete them. After you ask us to delete your account, we delete your data from our systems within 30 days, except what we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Delete your data

Email dave@prolynk.io with the subject "Data deletion" from the email on your account. You can also ask for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Kids

ProLynk is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
